# Vault

Think of this as your account. A **Vault** is your personal, encrypted container in Anytype. Everything you create lives inside your Vault, stored directly on your device.&#x20;

Unlike other apps with accounts and passwords, no company holds the keys to your Vault on Anytype. It is protected by cryptography so strong that it's virtually impossible for the most powerful computers on the planet to break into it. It is highly secure.&#x20;

## Why it matters

Most apps store your data on their servers, and you trust them to keep it safe. In most cases, companies can access the content in your app because they have keys to your locks. This is not the case with Anytype.&#x20;

On Anytype, your Vault is encrypted on your device before anything is synced. You own your data. Only you, and the people you choose to share with, can read it.

This means your information stays private by default — not because of a company's privacy policy, but because of math. With end-to-end encryption, no third parties can view your data with your explicit permission—not even Anytype itself.

## How it works

Your Vault is created the first time you set up Anytype. Along with it, you receive a [key.md](key.md "mention")—think of it like a master password that replaces the standard email and password combination.&#x20;

* Your Key is a 12-word phrase that can never be changed or reset.&#x20;
* It is generated locally on your device and never shared with Anytype, which ensures that your data is protected.&#x20;
* Not requiring an email and password is what makes Anytype offline-first and decentralized. You don't need anybody's permission to create an account, use Anytype, and connect with others.&#x20;

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you lose your Key and are logged out on all devices, there is no way to recover your Vault because Anytype itself does not store it. Save your Key somewhere safe. See [Key](key.md) for details.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Your Vault syncs across your devices automatically when you're online. You can also work fully offline, as any changes will simply sync the next time you connect to a network.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
