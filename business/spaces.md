# Spaces

Owners and admins create and manage the organization's spaces in **Spaces** in the Admin Console. Each space is public or private.

| | Public | Private |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who's in it | Every member of the organization | Only people an owner or admin adds |
| Who sees it in the app | Everyone | Only its members |
| Permission | The space's default: Writer or Reader | Chosen when adding each person |

Owners and admins can see and manage all spaces in the Admin Console, including private ones. Whoever creates a space gets **Admin** in it.

## Create a space

1. Open **Spaces** and click **Create space**.
2. Enter a **Space name**.
3. Choose **Visibility**: **Public** or **Private**.
4. For a public space, choose the **Default permission for joiners**: **writer** (can create and edit) or **reader** (view only).
5. Click **Create**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
You can't change visibility after the space is created.
{% endhint %}

## Public spaces

Every member is added to a public space automatically, both when the space is created and when someone new joins the organization. Members can leave and rejoin.

Use public spaces for company-wide content, such as a wiki or announcements.

## Private spaces

Only an owner or admin can add people to a private space. Other members don't see it in the app.

Use private spaces for a team or project.

## Manage who's in a space

Open a space in **Spaces**.

* **Add members:** click **Add members**, pick people, choose a permission and click **Add**. **Reader** is selected by default.
* **Change permission:** set a person to **Reader**, **Writer** or **Admin**.
* **Remove:** select people, click **Remove selected** and confirm.

{% hint style="info" %}
Anyone who's a Writer or Admin in any space counts as an editor for billing.
{% endhint %}

## The General space

Every organization has a General space. **New members can edit in General** in [Organization Settings](org-settings.md#new-members) sets whether new members get edit or read-only access there.
